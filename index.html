Factory Show

For the past 12 years, Shandong Manshi Flooring Manufacturing Co., Ltd has been dedicated to the production and manufacturing of flooring. We have a spacious production base and advanced production lines to ensure the high quality of SPC, laminate flooring and solid wood flooring. We combine innovative engineering with aesthetic design to provide environmentally friendly, durable and customizable flooring solutions to 60+ countries around the world. We focus on mid‑to‑high‑end customers and offer OEM and ODM services to our clients. We welcome all customers to visit our factory. We look forward to having face‑to‑face communication with our customers. If you have any questions about flooring, please feel free to contact us.
